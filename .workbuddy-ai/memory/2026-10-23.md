## Follow-up: ADX priming bug (found in review)

- `adx_trend` consumed its warmup priming flag on the first readable ADX bar even when ADX
  was below threshold (bar 27 on BTCIRT_60 had ADX 10.9), leaving the strategy with 0 entries
  for the whole session. Fixed: the flag is only consumed once `ADX >= adx_threshold`.
- Result after fix: BTCIRT_60 +9.5% (Sharpe 3.86, DD -1.8%), XRPIRT_60 +15.1%, ETHIRT_60 -2.0%,
  one entry each. Regression test: `test_adx_trend_waits_for_strength_before_priming`.

## Carry sleeve (completes the portfolio: 2 bets, not 1)

- Data: `scripts/fetch_carry_dataset.py` pulls Hyperliquid funding + candles (paginated,
  cached in gitignored `data/crypto/funding/`). 120d x BTC/ETH/SOL: 2880 funding events each,
  mean APR 8.49/8.47/7.01%, positive hours 91.9/91.3/85.5%.
- Hyperliquid funding is pinned to the 0.01%/8h baseline (1.25e-5/hr): p50..p95 are all that
  value. Thresholds above it never trigger; a threshold *at* it chatters.
- `FundingCarryAlgorithm`: added `signal_window_hours` (smoothed rate, no action until the
  window is full) + `min_hold_hours`. Raw-rate version traded 115x/120d and lost 7.4% to fees;
  smoothed 72h + 48h hold trades once and keeps the carry.
- `run_funding_carry_backtest(..., hedge_with_spot=True)`: delta-neutral (short perp + long spot).
- **Bug fixed:** the engine credited the entry fee but never the short-sale proceeds to cash, so
  any short marked the account down by its full notional. All earlier naked-carry numbers
  (-98% returns, -118% DD) were artefacts.
- **Bug fixed:** `fetch_hyperliquid_funding_range` must page *forward*; the public endpoint
  returns the oldest 500 rows of the window, so walking backwards returned only page one.
- Measured carry (hedged, 1x): BTC 9.51% APR, ETH 10.92%, SOL 2.83%, DD -0.19%/-0.32%.
- Correlation (daily, 120d): trend<->trend 0.68-0.80 (one bet); carry<->trend -0.14..+0.11.
  Aggregated trend vs carry rho = -0.08.
- Book (65% trend / 35% carry, weekly WF): 27.44% annualized, vol 15.57%, Sharpe 1.63, DD -4.49%.
  At equal risk (1.55x) it annualizes 42.52% vs 35.95% trend-only => +6.57 pp.
- Intraday reversion sleeve **rejected on evidence**: gross return (zero costs) -8.7%..+4.2%,
  mean ~-2% at 5m/15m on HL perps. Not a cost problem — there is no signal. Also HL caps
  intraday history at ~5000 candles (5m ~17d, 15m ~52d).
- Allocator additions: `daily_returns_from_equity_curve`, `align_on_common_dates`,
  `apply_weight_caps`, `PortfolioConfig.vol_floor` / `weight_caps`.
- Single-linkage clustering cannot see that 3 carry sleeves are one bet (cross-rho 0.08-0.11 is
  noise around a near-constant drift) — aggregate by sleeve kind before allocating.

  and `tests/test_lab_security.py::test_lab_api_requires_token_for_mutations` (unauth mutation
